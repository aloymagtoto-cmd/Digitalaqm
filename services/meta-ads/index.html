AQM Digital Marketing / Services / Meta Ads

Meta ads
management.

Creative built against why people actually buy, not another payday-sale asset — and conversion tracking that survives iOS.

Meta rewards creative volume, but volume without a thesis is just expensive noise. The accounts that work are the ones where each ad answers a specific reason someone buys — and where the copy came from real customer objections rather than a brainstorm.

Underneath that sits the boring half: the Pixel, the Conversions API, event deduplication, and a catalogue that actually matches your inventory. Since iOS 14 that plumbing is the difference between an account that scales and one that stalls.

I run prospecting and retargeting as one system — typically a 70/30 split — so retargeting doesn't quietly take credit for demand prospecting created.

03 — How it runs

The order
matters.

Buying against a broken signal just spends the budget faster, so the measurement work always comes first.

Get a free account review →
  1. 01

    Fix the signal

    Pixel, Conversions API, event dedup, catalogue match. Meta's algorithm is only as good as the events you feed it.

  2. 02

    Map the motives

    Interview the sales inbox. Every recurring objection is an ad concept, and every motive gets its own creative.

  3. 03

    Test at the concept level

    Rotate concepts, not just headlines. Changing the button colour on a bad idea produces a slightly cheaper bad idea.

  4. 04

    Split prospecting and retargeting

    Hold the ratio deliberately so you can see which half is actually generating demand.

04 — Questions

Before you ask.

Do you produce the creative as well?

I write the copy and direct the concepts, and I build static and simple motion assets. For heavier video production I'll work with your team or brief an editor — the thinking about what the ad needs to say stays with me either way.

Does click-to-messenger work outside the Philippines?

It works anywhere Messenger and WhatsApp have real penetration, and it suits considered purchases where people have questions before buying — vehicle builds, services, high-ticket retail. For simple ecommerce checkouts, standard conversion campaigns usually win.
