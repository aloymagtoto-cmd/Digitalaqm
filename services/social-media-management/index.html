AQM Digital Marketing / Services / Social media

Social media
management.

Organic social that gives the paid media something worth amplifying — and a DM inbox that turns interest into a sale instead of leaving it on read.

Most social media management is measured on the wrong things. Followers and engagement rate feel like progress, but neither appears in a revenue report. A post that gets 400 likes and no enquiries has done nothing except cost you a morning.

The version that works treats organic as a testing ground for paid. Formats and hooks get proven cheaply on the feed, and whatever earns attention organically becomes ad creative with a budget behind it. That loop is why the two disciplines belong with the same person — when the social manager and the media buyer are different agencies, the learning never crosses over.

The other half is the inbox. Comments and DMs are where considered purchases actually close, and most brands treat them as an afterthought.

03 — How it runs

The order
matters.

Buying against a broken signal just spends the budget faster, so the measurement work always comes first.

  1. 01

    Audit what already lands

    Look at the last six months of posts against enquiries, not likes. The formats that quietly produce conversations are usually not the ones getting the most engagement.

  2. 02

    Set the pillars

    Three or four repeatable formats tied to real buying questions, so production stops starting from a blank page every week.

  3. 03

    Run the inbox

    Comments and DMs handled daily, with recurring questions logged — that log becomes next quarter's content and the next round of ad copy.

  4. 04

    Promote the winners

    Anything that earns attention organically gets budget behind it, tested properly as paid creative.

04 — Questions

Before you ask.

Do you actually post, or just plan?

Both. I build the calendar, produce the assets from what you already have, and schedule and publish. If you have an in-house designer or videographer, I brief them instead and keep the strategy and scheduling.

Do you handle comments and DMs?

Yes, and it's usually where the money is. For considered purchases — services, vehicle work, higher-ticket retail — the sale closes in the inbox, not the caption. Anything needing your specialist knowledge gets routed to you rather than guessed at.

Can I hire you for organic social only?

You can, though it's rarely the best value. The reason this works is that organic and paid inform each other; running only one half means paying for tests whose results nobody applies. If budget is tight, the honest advice is usually to fix tracking first, then paid, then organic.
