App Store Optimisation
& iOS UA.

Paid installs that convert because the keyword, the ad, and the product page are telling the same story.

A high tap-through rate with a low install rate means the store listing is writing cheques the product page can't cash. A 46% tap-to-install rate — nearly half of everyone who taps — is what it looks like when keyword intent and product page actually match.

That match is the whole job. Efficiency on Apple Search Ads comes from buying the right intent, not from buying more volume and hoping the funnel holds.

Beyond the install, campaigns get split by funnel stage — install, trial, subscribe — so you can see which step is actually costing you money.

04 — Questions

Before you ask.

Do you work on Android too?

Yes — Google App Campaigns and Play Store listing work. Most of my recent portfolio work has been iOS-first, which is where the attribution constraints are hardest, but the same funnel logic applies on Android.

Can you improve organic installs, or only paid?

Both, and they compound. Apple Search Ads search-term data is the best keyword research available for the App Store, so paid campaigns directly inform the listing changes that lift organic visibility.
